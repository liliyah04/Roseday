To my Rose

There are some people you meet, and then there are people who quietly become a part of you.

I don't really know how to begin this without sounding ridiculously sentimental, so I suppose I'll just be honest and let myself be a little ridiculous. You matter to me. A lot.

And I don't mean that in the simple way of saying “you're my friend” and leaving it there. You became something much more familiar than that. Somewhere along the way, talking to you stopped feeling like something I had to make time for and started feeling like something that naturally belonged in my day.

Something happens and my brain immediately goes, “I need to tell Rose.” Something funny happens and I want you to know. Something annoying happens and I want to complain to you. Something exciting happens and somehow telling you makes it feel even more exciting.

You became one of my favourite places to be myself.

I think that's one of the things I love most about us. I don't have to carefully construct every sentence before I send it. I don't have to wonder whether I'm being too loud, too weird, too excited, too dramatic, too random, or just completely ridiculous.

I can simply exist.

And somehow, with you, that has always felt enough.

“Thank you for making being myself feel easy.”

You understand parts of me that I don't always know how to explain. You let me talk about the tiniest things as if they matter, and you somehow make the biggest things feel a little less heavy.

I can tell you things without rehearsing them first. I can say the thought exactly as it appears in my head, even when it makes absolutely no sense, and somehow you still understand what I meant.

That kind of comfort is rare. And I don't take it for granted.

So today, on your birthday, I don't just want to wish you a happy birthday and disappear behind a pretty little cake emoji.

I want you to know that somewhere in this enormous, chaotic world, there is a person who is genuinely grateful that you exist.

Someone who notices your little things. Someone who remembers the conversations that probably meant nothing to you but somehow stayed with me. Someone who would happily listen to another completely unnecessary story because, well... it's yours.

And if you ever wonder whether you are loved, appreciated, remembered, or important — I hope you remember this page.

Not because a website can prove anything. But because I made this entire little world just to remind you of something very simple:

You are very, very easy to love.

Not because you're perfect. Not because you always know what to say. Not because every day with you is some beautiful movie scene.

But because you're you.

My baby. My darling. My favourite person to tell things to. My safe little corner of chaos.

My Rose.

with all my love
always,
