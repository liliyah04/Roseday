if i had to explain you

Things I adore about you.

I don't think I could ever make a proper list. There are too many little things. The way you understand me. The way I can tell you absolutely anything without stopping to wonder if you'll judge me.

And perhaps my favourite thing is this: whenever something happens — something funny, exciting, stupid, terrible, amazing — my first thought is somehow always “I need to tell Rose.”

You are one of the very few people I never have to rehearse myself for.

and that means more than you know
01

You understand me.

Not just the things I say out loud. Somehow, you understand the things behind them too. I never feel like I have to translate myself for you.

02

I can be completely me.

I can ramble, brag, complain, overshare, say something completely ridiculous and then immediately tell you something serious. There is no careful version of me required.

03

You feel safe.

There are things I don't tell everyone. Things I keep tucked away because I don't know how they'll be received. With you, I somehow want to open that little locked door.

04

You are my first thought.

Something happens and before I even realise it, my brain is already going: “Wait. I have to tell Rose this.” That probably says everything.

05 · the biggest one

You make being understood feel effortless.

And I don't think you realise how rare that is. To have someone you can talk to without measuring every sentence. Someone who can hear the messy, unfiltered version and still stay. Someone who makes you think, “Oh. I can tell her this.”

That's one of the most precious things you've ever given me.
