Happy Birthday. my Rose

If this little world had a thousand more pages, I would still find a thousand more reasons to write about you.

my baby. my darling. my everything.
the last letter
My Rose,

Some people are simply unforgettable.

And then there are people like you, who somehow become part of the little things without even trying.

The person I want to tell things to. The person I can be completely ridiculous with. The person I can talk to without rehearsing every word first.

I hope you know how deeply loved you are. Not because of what you do for people, not because of how much you give, but simply because you are you.

So today, I hope you laugh too loudly, smile until your cheeks hurt, and let yourself feel just how special this day is.

Happy birthday, Rose. I hope this year is gentle with you, kind to you, and full of little moments that make you think, “yeah, life is pretty beautiful.”

always, your person
before you leave

You are loved.

More than these pages could ever hold. More than this little website could ever say.

For Rose,

on her birthday · and every ordinary day after it
